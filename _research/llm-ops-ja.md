Can an LLM help operators build and run a network? At Interop Tokyo ShowNet, a demonstration network of 21 racks of heterogeneous devices, 105 network engineers used an LLM-based chatbot during construction and operation. The chatbot could retrieve domain documents (RAG), drive device CLIs, and read the ticket system. Participants rated its responses: 68.1% of the evaluations were positive. The chat histories also show that knowing what the chatbot can do leads to better answers.
