How helpful a Large Language Model (LLM) is in network operations
varies with the model, the environment, the people who use it, and the
harness around it, including the tools available to the LLM.

In this work, we built an LLM-based chatbot specialized for building
and operating networks, and had more than 100 network engineers
actually use and evaluate it while building and operating Interop
Tokyo ShowNet in 2025.
